🏦 CreditPulse: Bank Loan Portfolio & Risk Intelligence




🏛️ Project Overview

CreditPulse is a high-performance financial analytics solution designed to monitor the lending health of a banking institution. By analyzing a dataset of 38,500+ loan applications, this project provides a 360-degree view of portfolio performance, borrower behavior, and credit risk. The dashboard tracks critical KPIs such as funded amounts, interest rates, and Debt-to-Income (DTI) ratios to ensure data-driven risk management.

High-Impact Findings

• Regional Demand Concentration: Identified New York (NY) and California (CA) as the highest volume regions, suggesting a need for localized marketing and risk models.

• Borrower Intent: Debt consolidation emerged as the primary driver for loan applications, accounting for the largest share of the portfolio.

• Liquidity Tracking: Monitored a total of $474.7M in received payments, ensuring a clear view of the institution's cash flow and recovery efficiency.




🛠️ Technical Architecture

Data Pipeline & Methodology

1. Data Ingestion: Processed 38,500+ granular loan records using Microsoft Excel.

2. Advanced Modeling: Utilized Pivot Tables and Power Query to aggregate data by loan status, grade, and purpose.

3. KPI Engineering: Developed custom formulas to track Month-over-Month (MoM) growth in applications and funding.

4. Visualization: Crafted a professional executive dashboard with interactive filters for regional and temporal analysis.

Tech Stack

• Analysis Engine: Microsoft Excel (Advanced)

• Data Modeling: Pivot Tables & Power Query

• Visualizations: Dynamic Charts & Slicers

• Metrics Focus: Credit Risk & Portfolio Health
